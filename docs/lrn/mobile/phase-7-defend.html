The asks, by room (statewide, not one valley)

APSAC
  • glacial-lake and wetland maps (NWIA), bulk
  • 2.5 m imagery access (SISDP-U)
  • full-resolution land-cover sources
  • LiDAR for priority districts (they have the skill)
GSI / PWD / BRO
  • dated landslide records with places
  • road-block logs (PWD, BRO, PMGSY): the best route to more dated slides
  • post-disaster study reports
CWC / dam operators
  • danger levels not yet published (7 of our 32 have official ones)
  • river cross-sections → depth
  • reservoir levels and release schedules
🗣️ Frame it: "You'd be giving records to your own forecast. Lined up on the map, they sharpen every product for your district, and the terms stay yours."
