🎮 what each Guild guards

Every Guild at the table has spent years on one thing, and it's protective of it. The Sky-Eye Guild (APSAC) makes maps, so it spots false sharpness at once. The Rock Guild (GSI) made the landslide records we learned from, so it wants them used carefully. The River Guild (CWC) owns the height sticks and the red lines, so it watches how we treat them.

Before answering, ask yourself: what is this Guild guarding? Then answer that worry first.

APSAC: false precision, how maps were checked, satellites and clouds. GSI: misuse of its inventory, timing from few dated slides, thresholds. CWC: its danger levels and gauges, bad readings, skill against "the river stays put".
An APSAC scientist says: "Your map is 100 m, but your rain is 11 km." What's the worry, and the one-line answer?
The worry is false precision. "Two halves: the ground is 100 m, the rain is at rain scale, about 11 km in the history and 22 km live. The fine detail you see is the ground, not the weather, and the app says so."

"Not quite: 0.86 is a ranking score. Show it a place that slid and one that didn't, on ground it never saw, and it picks the right one about 6 times in 7. It isn't a percentage of correct answers, and it isn't a chance."

5Your limits card: every limit with what lifts it

Never say a free-tier limit as if it were permanent. Name the tier that lifts it, and keep physics apart from budget.

Every lock has a key. Some keys are Free (we can do it ourselves), some need an Ask (a letter to a Guild), some are Paid. And a few locks are physics: no key exists, like seeing through monsoon cloud with a camera.

🎮 every lock has a key

Never say a limit as if it were forever. Say what lifts it. "Live rain is about 22 km" is a budget lock: a paid plan or a bigger cloud fetch lifts it. "Camera satellites can't see through monsoon cloud" is a physics lock: no money lifts it, so we use radar instead.

Each limit is tagged Free / Ask / Paid and physics / budget. Never present a free-tier limit as a permanent ceiling.
Is the ~22 km spacing of the live rain a permanent limit?
No, it's a budget limit. Fetching all 892 rain points in the daily cloud run, or a paid weather plan, makes it finer. A valley cloudburst between points is a different, physics limit: that needs ground or radar rain in that valley.

6Never say → say instead

🚫 Cheat codes are phrases that sound good but lose an expert's trust in one second. Each one has a safe swap.

🎮 the cheat codes

In a game, a cheat code feels like winning. In the Council, it's how you lose. "90% accurate", "this area is safe", "better than the official forecast": each one makes an expert stop listening. The swaps say the same thing honestly.

Thirteen never-say phrases with what to say instead: accuracy, "safe", chances, "any state", sensors and alerts, the loop, "better than official", unique data, the high-river fault, SDI, "environmental".
Why is "this area is safe" a cheat code?
Because Very Low isn't zero: slides there have been rare (about 0.22× the usual), not impossible. And "safe" sounds like a warning decision, which belongs to the authority. Say: "Very Low: landslides have been rare on ground like this."
