1Who they are, and the family around them

APSAC (Arunachal Pradesh Space Application Centre, formerly SRSAC) is the state's nodal agency for remote sensing, GIS and GPS for every department (notification of 29 Feb 2016). It sits under the state's Department of Science & Technology.

NRSC (ISRO)runs Bhuvan. Partner on SILAAS (landslides) and SISDP-U (1:10K maps). Our 2014/2017/2023 landslide sets came via Bhuvan.
SAC (ISRO)runs VEDAS. Partner on NWIA (wetlands and glacial lakes).
IIRS, DehradunISRO's training institute, listed on APSAC's site.
APSACItanagar · ~20 staff, 10+ scientists
APSSDI — the state spatial data portal APSAC runs. Our rock-type and fault maps come from its open map server.
Data centre: 6 servers, 38 TB · LiDAR processing (no scanner) · camera drones, 6 drone ports
NESAC (Umiam)NE space centre. Runs NESDR (land-cover feed we found). Runs NE flood early warning: operational in Assam; Arunachal status unknown.
DST, Govt of AP · NECAPSAC's parent department (sanction route) · funder of most NE space projects.
APSDMA / DDMAsA separate department: who acts on a warning.

How much the geology really matters

weight: rock type + faults
13.8%
AUC lost without rock type
−0.004

Big in the model, small in the score. The three APSSDI inputs carry 13.8% of the model's use (by SHAP; rock type alone takes 23% of split gain). But terrain carries much the same signal, so removing the two rock-type inputs moves AUC only from 0.859 to 0.855 (MODEL_SUSCEPTIBILITY.md §6). Say this honestly if asked "how much do you depend on us?".

🔓 How we got it: the APSSDI catalogue needs a login, but its map server (apssdi.in/geoserver/ows) answers openly. Fetched 28 Jul 2026 and credited in the app. Nothing behind a login was bypassed. Two traps then: a broken certificate chain (fixed on their side by 22 Oct) and the server dropping offline for about 30 minutes at a time.

🏥 The hidden gem: APSSDI's health layer. 1,006 facilities, open now, each with beds, ambulance (572 yes), blood bank (229), emergency care (677), operating theatre (288), oxygen and X-ray. Today we hold 48 health sites from OpenStreetMap. It turns "this road may be blocked" into "the nearest hospital with an operating theatre may be cut off".
🛣️ The best fit for a new SlideSense task: their route-alignment work. APSAC already plans routes for roads and power lines (a drone-surveyed Likabali–Siji line; border roads Tato–Mechuka and Zido–Singha). Adding our landslide hazard to their route choices is a joint product, not a competitor.

8What we offer back

Their maps answer where. Ours adds when, every day, on the same ground.

live
A daily "when" layer, statewide
7-day landslide outlook for any place or road; flood chances at 32 gauged rivers, plus an area outlook.
live
A public score sheet
Every river forecast written down before the day and checked against the gauge.
ready
An independent score of their maps
The same test we ran on GSI's map: ranking, capture at equal area, where maps disagree.
built as files
The Data Backbone as APSSDI layers
29 sources on one 100 m grid, served the way APSSDI already serves layers.
planned
Field data in their SILAAS format
Any field visit logs slides and flood marks in FLIM-compatible fields.
when funded
Survey work for their drone team
If a client funds detailed terrain, APSAC flies it instead of an outside vendor.
🧰 Fixes we found, to hand over politely: settlements miss Itanagar and 5 HQ towns · SILAAS report/map/dataset links return 404 · many menu pages 404 (landslide, cryosphere, disaster management) · the map server dropped offline for ~30 minutes at a time in Jul–Aug. (The certificate fault is already fixed.)
