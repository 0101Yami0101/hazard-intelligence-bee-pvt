## 0 · The approach in short

1. **Put the whole vision in, and label every item by where it stands.** Four labels (§1).
   Every output uses the same labels and the same wording.
2. **Big vision, realistic commitments.** The programme window (Nov 2026 – May 2027) lists
   only what the grant (up to ₹20 L) and the team can deliver. Everything bigger goes under
   "beyond the programme", where it can be as ambitious as we like.
3. **Proof stays proof.** The Proof of Work PDF shows evidence first. The roadmap pages come
   after a clear divider.
4. **Detail in the PDFs, the short version in the 12 answers.** Answers 2, 4 and 9 have only
   14–34 words left under the 500 limit. The answers carry a compact labelled list and point
   to the PDFs.

**Why the labels make the claim stronger, not weaker.**

- The judging headings include **maturity, feasibility** and (in the USPR) **resource
  realism** and **validation evidence**.
- Experts will open the demo link and will see what is really there.
- The declaration says the details are correct.
- Selection leads to a pilot that tests every claim.

A labelled roadmap gets full credit for the vision and keeps the credibility of everything
that is built. The PDF already promises "nothing on this page is a promise dressed as a
feature"; that becomes a selling point.

## 1 · The four labels

| Label | Means | Wording | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Built** | Working in the app on real Assam data, and checked | Present tense | "SoilSense ranks 703 catchments, each with its main cause" |
| **Being tested** | Built, and judged against a pass mark set beforehand. Adopted only if it passes | "being tested against …; adopted only if it passes" | AI erosion map (AD-01) |
| **In the programme (month)** | A committed deliverable with a month and what it depends on | "will deliver by Feb 2027, once …" | Field form round trip (AD-12) |
| **Beyond the programme** | Designed for, but needs pilot results, funding or partners | "designed to extend to …" | Other North-East states (AD-35) |

**Wording rules.**

- Nothing future in the present tense.
- "AI" only for the parts that learn from examples. The formula is called a formula.
- No partner is named as a partner without their agreement. Write "we will approach".
- No government data is assumed. Write "requested; the plan works without it".
- Tonnes are always "indicative". We lead with ranks.
- A pass mark is stated wherever a test is mentioned.

### 2.1 Built today

- Erosion-risk map, all Assam, 100 m, six classes, with "not assessed" land.
- Priority list: 703 catchments and 35 districts, each with its cause, a second reason,
  typical measures and how firm its rank is.

  weeks.

  land-degradation map.
